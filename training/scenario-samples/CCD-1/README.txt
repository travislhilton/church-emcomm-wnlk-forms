Church of Jesus Christ EMCOMM Winlink training set CCD-1: Copper Creek Dam failure, Larkspur Stake
==============================================================================

Acronyms used below: EMCOMM (emergency communications), EOC (Emergency Operations Center), HAM
(amateur radio operator), ERD (Emergency Response Director), WSRM and AWSRM (the Welfare and Self-Reliance
Manager, then the Area Welfare and Self-Reliance Manager), ICS (Incident Command System), FRS (Family Radio
Service), VARA (the sound-card radio modem Winlink uses).

Independent volunteer project, not an official Church product. Generated by
installer/make-scenario-samples.py from training/scenario-samples-src/copper-creek-dam.json.

The drill: Winlink training set for the Larkspur Stake EOC (Emergency Operations Center) on the Copper Creek Dam drill (Sunday 21 March, 07:50, +4 C and rain; evacuation order for south Larkspur at 07:50; the dam fails at 08:28 and the water reaches south Larkspur at 09:00; power and cell service gone in the south from 09:05; the stake at CONTINGENCY (radio) from 09:10). The Juniper Plains Area, the Larkspur Valley, every unit, person, phone number (555-555-01xx), email (@churchemcomm.sim), street address and the Larkspur City EOC are invented. The city EOC's Winlink call sign is set on the trainee's station. Independent volunteer project, not an official Church product.

What is here (24 messages, numbered in drill-clock order so you can send them in sequence):
  * 16 Church forms (Disaster Report, Supply Order, Work Crew Log) from the stake's wards and
    branches to the Larkspur Stake EOC (Emergency Operations Center). THIS IS AN EXERCISE is ticked.
  * 8 ICS 213RR Resource Requests on Winlink's standard form, between the stake EOC and the
    Larkspur City EOC at Fire Station 1, Center Street, Larkspur. The city does not run this system: it gets only
    ICS forms, never Church forms. Its Winlink call sign is the one set on your stations.

Where they are after installing (HAM Field Operator and EOC Operator roles):
  Winlink Express -> New Message -> Select Template -> Global Templates ->
    Church of Jesus Christ EMCOMM Training CCD-1 -> pick one.
  The ICS 213RR forms are built from your station's own standard ICS213RR form when the installer runs;
  a station without Winlink's standard templates gets only the Church forms (send the ICS requests by hand
  from the text below with Winlink's ICS USA Forms -> ICS213RR).
  A copy of the set is in Documents\Church of Jesus Christ EMCOMM\Training\Winlink Training CCD-1\.

Use: open the form, check it, click Submit, address the message (Church forms to your stake EOC's Winlink
address; ICS requests to the receiving EOC's address), and send by Telnet, VARA FM or VARA HF. On the stake EOC
the Church forms arrive as EXERCISE reports; an ICS 213RR from the city's call sign arrives as a work item
named for the city. Answer each ICS 213RR with an ICS 213 General Message, as the context line asks.

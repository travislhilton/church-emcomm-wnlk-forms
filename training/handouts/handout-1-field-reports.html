Field reports that get through, no EOC needed

No EOC (Emergency Operations Center) is required. Fill in the Church's Disaster Report, Supply Order or Work Crew Log on any phone, with or without signal. It reaches the Team Leader, the Emergency Response Director (ERD) or the stake by text or email, by a HAM (amateur radio) operator's Winlink message, or on paper.

1Before the emergency (5 minutes)

  • Install the app, with internet (a bookmark does not work offline). iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: Chrome → Install app.
  • Open each form once from the app.
  • Load your stake's Comms Sheet (Comms sheet → Load sheet file): who to send to.
  • Print 3 blank forms of each (Paper forms) for the go-bag.

2Fill in a report

  1. Open the Church of Jesus Christ EMCOMM app, or scan a QR (Quick Response) poster.
  2. Tap Disaster Report, Supply Order or Work Crew Log.
  3. Fill it in. Your GPS (Global Positioning System) location is captured for you.
  4. No signal? Tap Save to Outbox. Save as many as you need; each keeps its own GPS.

3Send it by the path that works: PACE (Primary, Alternate, Contingency, Emergency)

P

Cell signal: tap Share by text or email to your Team Leader, ERD or the stake.

A

Any internet anywhere (home, shelter, business, meetinghouse, Starlink): the same Share.

C

Radio only: Save to My Phone, give the file to a HAM operator (a USB (Universal Serial Bus) thumb drive, AirDrop, Nearby Share); it goes by Winlink.

E

Nothing connects: Print it (a printer on the same Wi-Fi) or copy it onto a blank form; hand it to the Crew Chief or a runner.

Keep the report in your Outbox too: it can still go in when a path comes back.

4HAM operator: send it by Winlink

  1. Winlink Express → New Message → Select Template → Global Templates → Church of Jesus Christ EMCOMM Forms → the form.
  2. Load Data → the volunteer's .txt file: every field fills in. Or type in a report given by voice or on paper.
  3. Drill? Tick THIS IS AN EXERCISE. Submit, address it from the Comms Sheet, Open Session: Telnet on internet; on radio, VARA FM to the nearest RMS (Radio Message Server) gateway.

5Receiving it: no EOC needed

The message text is the report: plain text, nothing extra on the air. Winlink Express opens it as the filled-in form, with Print, Copy and Save.

Read as ordinary email (by the welfare line, say), it is the same report as text.

When the stake opens an EOC, the same reports file there by themselves (handout 2).

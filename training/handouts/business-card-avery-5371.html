Business cards: Avery 5371 (2 × 3.5 in, 10 per sheet)

Page 1 is the front (Church of Jesus Christ EMCOMM: emergency communications), page 2 the back (HAM Sidekick Station: for amateur radio nets). Each QR (Quick Response) code opens that project's guide on GitHub.
  1. Print one test on plain paper first: Actual size / 100 % (never "Fit to page"), Letter, two-sided, flip on long edge. Hold it over a card sheet against a window: the cards should sit inside the perforations.
  2. Then load the Avery sheet as your printer's manual asks (often face down in the rear or manual tray), paper type Cardstock / Heavy, and print.
  3. No two-sided printer? Print page 1, put the sheet back in the tray turned over (top edge first, as for your printer), print page 2.

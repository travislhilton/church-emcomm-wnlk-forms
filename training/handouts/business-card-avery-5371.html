Business cards: Avery 5371 (2 × 3.5 in, 10 per sheet)

Page 1 is the front (Church of Jesus Christ EMCOMM: emergency communications), page 2 the back (HAM Sidekick Station: for amateur radio nets). Each QR (Quick Response) code opens that project's guide on GitHub.
  1. Line it up once. Click Print the alignment test and print it on plain paper: Actual size / 100 % (never "Fit to page" or "Scale to fit"), Letter, two-sided, flip on long edge, from this page in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Check the 5 in line at the bottom with a ruler. Shorter or longer means the printer scaled the page: fix the scale setting first.
  3. Lay the test over an Avery sheet, the same side up, and hold both against a window. Where a perforation crosses a scale, read how far the printed line is from it (each tick is 1/32 in, the long ticks 1/8 in). Line too far right or low: enter a negative number. Too far left or high: positive. Do it for the front, then turn both over for the back.
  4. Print the test again until the lines sit on the perforations. The numbers stay in this browser.
  5. Then load the Avery sheet as your printer's manual asks (often face down in the rear or manual tray), paper type Cardstock / Heavy, and print.
  6. No two-sided printer? Print page 1, put the sheet back in the tray turned over (top edge first, as for your printer), print page 2.

PACE Levels: Which Path, When to Step Down

PACE Situation Winlink Connection
P Cell + internet up Volunteers text/email files to the EOC. You: Open Session → Telnet
A Cell down, any internet anywhere Any connection that still reaches the internet. Files arrive by email or USB drive. Open Session → Telnet
C No internet; a gateway answers Data: Open Session → Vara FM Winlink → [pre-programmed VHF/UHF gateway freq] (very/ultra high frequency). Send as Winlink Message. A gateway with no internet may hold it for the CMS (Common Message Servers).
Voice: stake VHF/UHF net (repeater, then simplex); volunteers on FRS/GMRS (Family Radio Service / General Mobile Radio Service) to Team Leaders.
C “No CMS available” or no gateway Wrong Send as stays in the Outbox. 1. Vara FM RMS Post Office: Send as Post Office. 2. Vara FM P2P (peer-to-peer): Send as Peer-to-Peer. Not forwarded: the recipient must connect. 3. Vara HF Winlink (high frequency), 40m or 80m: only what must go. iPhone reports: a USB-C/Lightning thumb drive in the go-kit, or the EOC Wi-Fi Send to EOC.
E Nothing connects, no one answers the net Runner: the printed form, or a blank form filled by hand, to the EOC; the operator types it in. Hold saved files; send when any path returns.
Addresses, gateways, Post Office, P2P frequency, nets: from the stake Comms Sheet — printed copy in the go-bag, or load the sheet file on your phone at …/forms/comms.html.
PACE rule: step down a level after two failed attempts in 15 minutes, or when the Stake President decides it. Do not go back to a path that failed until a test message gets through. The stake Welfare and Self-Reliance Specialist (emergency communications) recommends the level, the Stake President decides, and the Emergency Response Director (ERD) or specialist announces it. The county may call ARES (Amateur Radio Emergency Service) operators to county duty at its Emergency level; agree beforehand who stays with the stake.

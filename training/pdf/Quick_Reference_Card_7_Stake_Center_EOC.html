WHO DECIDES

The stake Welfare and Self-Reliance Specialist (emergency communications) recommends the PACE (Primary, Alternate, Contingency, Emergency) level and when to open a Satellite EOC; the Stake President decides; the ERD (Emergency Response Director) or the specialist announces it. Set it at once: Setup tab → Jump to: PACE & SITREP (situation report).

SETUP TAB, ONCE

Level Stake · name · the wards it covers (Save EOC Identity adds them to the Roster) · parent = the two lines above the stake: the council line (Area Seventy; decisions) and the welfare line (the Welfare and Self-Reliance Manager, WSRM, then the Area Welfare and Self-Reliance Manager, AWSRM; supplies, approvals, reporting), or a Council / Area EOC when one is open · the SITREP (situation report) interval leadership set.
Comms sheet: every EOC’s Winlink address, gateways in order, net frequencies; Export sheet JSON (JavaScript Object Notation, a data file) to every Satellite EOC.

WHAT COMES IN

Every ward not covered by a satellite (phones, Winlink, Drop Folder; USB (Universal Serial Bus) drives too) and each Satellite EOC’s roll-up (merges itself; Via on the Messages tab shows which EOC it came through; nothing is counted twice).
Roster tab: wards and bishops, who has reported, who is overdue, missionaries not accounted for.

WHAT GOES UP, WITH EVERY SITREP

SITREP to the Stake President, the council line and the welfare line: first as soon as possible, then on the interval leadership set. PACE level in force in the header.
Supply Orders to the welfare line for approval; record the AWSRM’s answer on the order. Headquarters assistance requests go up the same way.
Export Roll-up JSON only to a Council / Area EOC when one is open (back of this card). Otherwise the SITREP is the stake’s report upward.

WORK ITEMS

The mission office, the county, the Stake President and the welfare line ask through this list; the reports add their own. Give every HIGH item an owner at once. Up = sent to the line above; write the decision when it comes back.
Card 3 runs the Message Center; start with its Overview checklist. This card: what your level does and owes the level above.

SITREP = situation report. The council line is the Area Seventy; the welfare line is the Welfare and Self-Reliance Manager (WSRM), then the Area Welfare and Self-Reliance Manager (AWSRM). A Council / Area EOC exists only when the council or the welfare region opens one.

ROLL-UP: HOW THE FILE TRAVELS

Setup tab → Export Roll-up JSON (“only new since last roll-up” ticked) → send it with every SITREP.
P / A (cellular, any internet): attach it to an email to the parent EOC’s drop email. C (radio): attach it to a Winlink message to the parent’s Winlink address. In order (VARA: the sound-card radio modem Winlink uses): RMS (Radio Message Server) Gateway, held at the gateway, VARA FM RMS Post Office, VARA FM P2P (peer-to-peer), then VARA HF. E (runner): on a USB drive with the printed SITREP.
The parent merges it by itself within 10 seconds (“Roll-up received from …”). From USB: drop it in the Drop Folder. Nothing counts twice.
Any device in the room, no app needed: scan the wall QR (Quick Response) code (or open http://<server-ip>:8080/m): new reports, Send to EOC, the Work items list (tap an item: Take it, In work, Up, Done; your name goes in its log). Paper by runner (Emergency): type it in at /forms/dr, /forms/so or /forms/wcl and tap Send; keep blank paper forms on hand (add ?blank=1, Print).
In a drill: one simulator per EOC. The purple drill bar on every screen says what this EOC plays, the clock or the start time, and who is ready: press I am ready. Every simulated report says SIMULATED; real reports still go through. New to drills? Read card 9.

1
A request arrives from the city’s call sign: the EOC files it as a work item named for the city.
2
Own it and decide: the Stake President or the Emergency Response Director says yes, how many, from which wards.
3
Answer with an ICS 213 General Message from Winlink’s ICS USA Forms: what you send, where, when, who leads.
4
Close the loop: the crew’s Work Crew Log comes back to the stake EOC as a Church form.
Keep the requests short and numbered. Never put Church forms, member names or ward details in a message to the city.

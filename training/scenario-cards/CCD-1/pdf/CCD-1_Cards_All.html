Copper Creek Dam: what has happened

THIS IS AN EXERCISE in an invented community (the Juniper Plains Area). Every form, message and net call says so. If anything real happens, anyone says “REAL WORLD” on the net or in the room and the drill stops. Never call 911 for the drill.

07:50, Sunday 21 March

  • Three days of warm rain on deep snow. Copper Creek Dam has seeped since last evening; at 07:40 the seep started carrying mud.
  • The sheriff orders Copper Ridge below Route 30 and the Copper Creek corridor through south Larkspur out now.
  • The Larkspur River bottoms from Center Street to Alder Flat are warned: get ready to leave.
  • +4 °C, light rain. Most members are getting ready for 9:00 sacrament meetings.
  • Phones work for now. Nobody knows how long the dam will hold.

Your first fifteen minutes

1
Your own family first. In the order zone: leave now, by the routes the sheriff gives.Then go to your assignment: the EOC, your ward’s gathering point, your radio.
2
Bishops and Team Leaders: account for members and missionaries in the zones.Who has left, who needs a ride, who will not leave.
3
HAM operators: check in on the stake net and stand by.The specialist recommends the PACE level (Primary, Alternate, Contingency, Emergency), the Stake President decides it, the net announces it.
4
EOC operators: open the Message Center, confirm the drill event and the level.First SITREP (situation report) as soon as you have a first assessment.

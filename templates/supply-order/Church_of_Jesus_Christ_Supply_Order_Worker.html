Emergency Supply Order
Worker Form
How to use this form:
1. Fill in the fields below. Your GPS (Global Positioning System) location fills itself in when the phone allows it.
2. On the EOC (Emergency Operations Center) Wi-Fi? Tap Send to EOC. The EOC has it in seconds and you see a green “received” screen. No files to save.
3. No signal where you are? Tap Save to Outbox. The report stays on this phone; save as many as you need. When you reach the EOC Wi-Fi, tap Send to EOC and scan the EOC QR (Quick Response) code on the wall.
4. Have cell service or internet? (Any Wi-Fi with internet counts — home, a shelter, a business, a neighbor, a meetinghouse, Starlink.) Tap Share by text or email and send the file straight to whoever you report to — your Team Leader, Emergency Response Director, Church leader, or your Stake or Satellite EOC (Emergency Operations Center).
5. Cannot reach the EOC at all? Tap Save to My Phone and get the file to an EMCOMM (emergency communications) HAM (amateur radio) operator any way you can — USB (Universal Serial Bus) drive, AirDrop, or Android Nearby Share (these work phone-to-phone with no signal). They load it into Winlink and send it to the Stake EOC by radio. The Stake EOC sends it up the welfare line (the Welfare and Self-Reliance Manager, WSRM, then the Area Welfare and Self-Reliance Manager, AWSRM).
6. Nothing works at all (Emergency)? Paper goes by hand. If a printer is on your Wi-Fi, or offers its own Wi-Fi (Wi-Fi Direct), tap Print and give the printed form to your Crew Chief or a runner. No printer? Copy the report onto a blank paper form and keep it in your Outbox too. Print blank forms before an event and keep a few with a pen in your go-bag.
Tick this for every drill, test or training report. The EOC (Emergency Operations Center) marks it as an exercise.

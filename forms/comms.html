PACE levels (Primary, Alternate, Contingency, Emergency) — which path to use

PPrimary — cell and internet up: text or email the form file
AAlternate — any surviving internet: Wi-Fi anywhere, Starlink
CContingency — radio: forms by Winlink over VHF/UHF (very high / ultra high frequency) with VARA FM (VARA: the sound-card radio modem Winlink uses), then HF (high frequency) with VARA HF; voice on the nets above
EEmergency — runner with the paper form (Print under the form, or a blank form filled by hand)

Rule: step down a level after two failed attempts in 15 minutes, or when the Stake President decides it on the specialist's recommendation. Step back up only after a test message round-trips.

Reports travel field → Satellite EOC (Emergency Operations Center) → Stake EOC → the council line (Area Seventy) and the welfare line (Welfare and Self-Reliance Manager, WSRM, then the Area Welfare and Self-Reliance Manager, AWSRM).

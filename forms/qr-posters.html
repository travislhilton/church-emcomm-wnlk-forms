Church of Jesus Christ EMCOMM (Emergency Communications) QR (Quick Response) Posters

Print and post these at staging areas, distribution points, and work sites.
Volunteers scan with their phone to open the correct form.

Ctrl+P / Cmd+P to print · Landscape orientation recommended for individual posters

These codes open the forms on the internet (GitHub Pages): the Primary path (cellular) and the Alternate path (any internet anywhere) of the PACE (Primary, Alternate, Contingency, Emergency) plan. A phone that opened them once keeps the forms offline. With no internet at all (Contingency and Emergency), scan the QR code on the EOC (Emergency Operations Center) wall display instead: it opens that EOC's own phone page on its Wi-Fi.

This sets the destination all QR codes point to. Make sure your repo is public and GitHub Pages is enabled.

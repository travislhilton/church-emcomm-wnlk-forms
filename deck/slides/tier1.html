Process Flow

1

Volunteer opens the app

Church of Jesus Christ EMCOMM app on the Home Screen, or a QR (Quick Response) code scan

2

Fill form, tap Save to Outbox

The report waits on the phone until it is sent

3

Tap Share / Send — text or email it straight to the EOC

To the Team Leader, ERD, or EOC Drop Folder address. At Primary, no HAM is needed

4

If a HAM received it instead: Winlink → Load Data → Submit

Opens template, clicks Load Data, selects .txt, reviews, submits via Telnet

5

Stake EOC: Drop Folder or Winlink auto-ingest → Message Center

Data appears in the correct tab; SITREP goes up to the AWSRM by email, forms by Winlink Telnet

Minimum Equipment

  • Volunteers: Any smartphone
  • HAM station: PC with Winlink Express + internet
  • EOC: Any laptop, browser, offline HTML (web page) file

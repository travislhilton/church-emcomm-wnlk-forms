VARA FM / HF: software modems, free at reduced speed (a license unlocks full speed). Emergency (below VARA HF): paper, a runner, the county liaison

The volunteer needs no special equipment — just a smartphone, plus an FRS/GMRS handheld for the voice lane at Contingency. Primary and Alternate need no radio license. Only Contingency radio requires HAM privileges. Invest first in Starlink (Alternate) and VHF/UHF with VARA FM (Contingency) since they cover most disaster scenarios; VARA HF is the last radio path, and below it the plan goes to paper and feet.

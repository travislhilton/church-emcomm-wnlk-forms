Key phrase on the net at every check-in: “Stake EOC, this is [callsign], level in force is [P / A / C / E], I have [N] forms.”

This is the slide to print for each role. Notice the volunteer row: three of the four cells are "fill it, save it, send it however you can." The switching logic lives with the Team Leader and the HAM. The leadership row is the same at every level because its job is to set the level, not to move messages — and it is set the way the Church's Emergency Communication Guidelines describe: the stake Welfare and Self-Reliance Specialist for emergency communications recommends the backup mode, the Stake President decides, and the ERD or the specialist announces it on the net. The last column matters more than it looks: Emergency is when the EOC may have to relocate, so the handoff export is part of the plan.

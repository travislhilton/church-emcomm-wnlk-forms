Volunteer Experience

Worker Form Process

For any volunteer — no HAM license, no app store, no account

1

Install the Church of Jesus Christ EMCOMM app — once, with internet

Open the portal (or scan the QR poster) → Add to Home Screen. No app store, no login. From then on it opens with no signal, even in airplane mode.

2

Fill in the form fields

Tap each field. Date pickers default to today. Location auto-fills from GPS (if permitted). All 3 forms mirror the official Church paper versions — fields are identical.

3

Tap “Save to Outbox”

The report waits in the app’s Outbox, kept even if the app is closed or the phone has no signal. Open it again to edit. Save to My Phone also writes a .txt backup copy.

4

Send it: “Send to EOC” or “Share”

On the EOC Wi-Fi: Send to EOC, scan the wall’s QR code; every Outbox report goes in, with a green receipt. Signal? Share by text or email to your Team Leader, ERD or the Stake EOC. No service? AirDrop or USB to a HAM operator, or read it on FRS/GMRS.

5

Your report reaches the EOC at any PACE level

P / A: your text or email lands in the EOC Drop Folder and appears in the EOC within 10 seconds. C: a HAM loads your .txt into Winlink and sends it by radio, or the EOC types in what you reported by voice. E: tap Print: the page is laid out like the Church paper form; or fill a blank one from your go-bag. A runner carries it.

Training time for volunteers: 15 minutes — one practice run before the event is all that is needed

The Worker Form is intentionally simple. It mirrors the paper form so volunteers already know what the fields mean. The key training points: (1) install the Church of Jesus Christ EMCOMM app from the portal before the event, while there is internet, so it opens with no signal; (2) Save to Outbox every report, and send the Outbox when you reach the EOC Wi-Fi or any signal; (3) when nothing connects, Print: the printout is laid out like the Church paper form, and the footer says it is based on that form and is not an official Church form.

Org Structure

Church Emergency Response Chain

Area Seventy

Regional Church leadership

AWSRM

Area Welfare & Self-Reliance Manager — receives supply orders & disaster reports; works with the Area Welfare & Self-Reliance Specialist (called by the Area Seventy) and the local WSRM

Welfare line

Stake President

Decides the PACE level in force; calls a stake Welfare & Self-Reliance Specialist (emergency communications) who recommends it

Bishop

Ward leadership — authorizes response operations

Emergency Response Director

Runs day-to-day operations from the Stake EOC, routing reports and supply requests upward

Files: Disaster Report (DR) · Supply Order (SO) · Work Crew Log (WCL)

Team Leader / Crew Chief

Field supervisor — collects worker form data, hands off to EMCOMM operator

Helping Hands Volunteers

Fill Worker Forms on phone or tablet — no HAM license required

EMCOMM Operators

HAM operators serve under the Stake President’s direction, through the stake specialist.

They are the radio bridge between field teams and the Stake EOC (Emergency Operations Center).

Data Flow

WCL → Crew Chief → ERD
SO → Team Leader → Stake EOC
DR → ERD → Stake EOC

Same flows at every PACE level — cell, Wi-Fi, Winlink radio, or runner. Stake EOC → council line and welfare line rides the same ladder.

Source: Church Area Planning Guide — providentliving.churchofjesuschrist.org

The chain of command mirrors the official Church structure, including the roles the Emergency Communication Guidelines name: a stake Welfare and Self-Reliance Specialist is called by and reports to the stake presidency; Area Welfare and Self-Reliance Specialists are called by the Area Seventy and coordinate closely with the AWSRM and the coordinating council; the local Welfare and Self-Reliance Manager is kept informed. Specialists recommend and coordinate backup modes under the direction of priesthood leaders — they never replace them. EMCOMM operators don't replace the chain — they provide the communication backbone for it. Every role in this chain interacts with the digital forms system in a specific way.

STARLINK

PACE: A · ALTERNATE

Cell + Internet Down — Satellite (Starlink) Works

Starlink: Orbital Signal, Ground Power Only

Why Starlink Survives

  • Signal comes from orbit — no local tower to destroy or lose power
  • Dish needs only ~100W — runs on a small battery bank or generator
  • After Hurricane Ian (2022): Starlink operational in southwest Florida within hours of landfall while cell was down for days
  • After Hurricane Helene (2024): FEMA (Federal Emergency Management Agency) deployed Starlink kits immediately to North Carolina

What Changes

  • Wired internet (cable/fiber) is down
  • Connect Winlink laptop to Starlink via Ethernet or Wi-Fi
  • Winlink Telnet works exactly as at Primary over Starlink — to the EOC and up to the welfare line
  • Volunteers on the Starlink Wi-Fi email the file themselves; Wi-Fi calling for voice
  • File transfer to HAM: USB, AirDrop, Nearby Share (Wi-Fi Direct)

Winlink connection: Telnet (no radio needed)

Starlink Power Options (100W draw)

Car / Truck

12V inverter from vehicle battery. Runs indefinitely while engine is on.

Portable Battery

100Wh battery pack → ~1 hr. 1kWh battery → ~10 hrs. Available at hardware stores.

Generator

Any small generator handles the 100W load. Run with Winlink laptop too (~200W total).

Solar Panel

200W panel charges a battery bank continuously. Viable for sustained operations.

Minimum Equipment

  • Volunteers: Phone (offline cache); USB drive
  • HAM / EOC station: Starlink dish + power source + PC + Winlink
  • Starlink: ~$599 hardware, ~$120/mo or emergency activation
  • No radio license needed at this level

Starlink is now standard kit for FEMA and first-responder deployments — consider adding one to the stake’s EMCOMM go-kit

Starlink at Alternate is the big gap most EMCOMM plans miss. When wired internet fails but you have Starlink and power, Winlink works exactly as at Primary, over Telnet — no radio required. A Starlink dish in the EMCOMM go-kit covers the space between internet loss and needing to spin up radio equipment.

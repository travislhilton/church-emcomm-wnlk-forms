Education & Readiness

Training Path by Role

Church-familiar language, minimal time commitment — workers need 5 minutes; leaders need an afternoon

Helping Hands
Volunteer

5 minutes to ready

  • Install the app from the portal (Add to Home Screen)
  • Fill one practice form; Save to Outbox
  • Share it to yourself by email or AirDrop
  • Print 3 blank forms for the go-bag

Day of: Open the app → fill → Save to Outbox → Send to EOC on its Wi-Fi, or text/email it. No service? Hand it to a HAM, or Print.

Deliver at: ward preparedness night or Helping Hands orientation

Team Leader
/ Crew Chief

1 hour

  • All Volunteer skills
  • Collect .txt files by AirDrop, USB, or email
  • Deliver files to the HAM station by any means
  • Monitor the ward FRS/GMRS channel; relay on the stake net
  • Keep a paper log of who reported

Key phrase up: “I have [N] reports for the HAM operator.”

Deliver at: ERD or Team Leader training meeting

EMCOMM
HAM Operator

4–6 hours + monthly net

  • Install Winlink + Church templates; test Telnet
  • Load Data from .txt → all 3 form types; send the 7 practice forms (THIS IS AN EXERCISE)
  • PACE switching: Telnet (A) → VARA FM (C) → HF (C)
  • Stake voice net check-in; VARA FM; nearest RMS gateways
  • One test message per connection type

Key phrase up: “Stake EOC, this is [callsign], level in force is [P/A/C/E], I have [N] forms.”

Deliver at: EMCOMM net or dedicated training session

EOC Operator
Satellite · Stake · Area

Half day + tabletop drill

  • Open the Message Center; load sample .mime and .txt files
  • Confirm each form type routes to the right tab
  • Generate a SITREP; send it to both lines above the stake at each PACE level
  • Work the Work Items list; log the level in force; shift handoff
  • Council / Area EOC: set the level, merge a stake roll-up, answer a Supply Order

Day of: Start EOC Server → open the URL → Setup tab → receive first message → set SITREP schedule and level in force.

Deliver at: stake EMCOMM training day or tabletop

“Maple Creek Flood” Tabletop — 2 Hours

Flash flood, 40 homes, power out, cell down. Round 1 (Alternate): forms over any Wi-Fi with internet, HAM by Telnet, SITREP emailed to both lines. Round 2 (Contingency): VARA FM radio, USB transfer, FRS/GMRS relayed on the stake net, SITREP to the welfare line by radio. Debrief: only the transport changed.

Monthly 15-Min Net

Roll call (2 min) → HAM sends one Winlink test message with dummy Worker Form data (5 min) → EOC confirms and reads back key fields (5 min) → gateway and equipment notes (3 min). Rotate PACE levels monthly; once a quarter the test message goes all the way to the welfare line (AWSRM). Log results.

Quick-Reference Cards

5×8" laminated cards, one per role (ten: one per EOC level, plus first drill and crew leader); print at home in black-and-white. Volunteer card has the QR code. HAM card has the PACE table. EOC cards have the SITREP routine and each level’s duties. Bishop’s 1-pager shows the whole system, PACE included, with no technical detail.

Training is layered: volunteers need 5 minutes; HAM operators need an afternoon plus monthly nets. Keep Church-familiar language throughout — Bishop, Stake President, Helping Hands — never ICS or NIMS (National Incident Management System) terms. The key insight for all roles: the forms, process, and chain of command never change — only the communication transport changes across PACE levels, and the tabletop is built to show exactly that.

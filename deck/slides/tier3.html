Winlink via VHF/UHF

HAM operator connects to a local Winlink RMS gateway on a 2 m or 70 cm FM (frequency modulation) radio with the VARA FM soundcard modem.

Typical range: 50–150 km to gateway · throughput up to 25 kbps with VARA FM · a full report is under 2 KB

Minimum Equipment

  • Volunteers: phone with form saved offline; USB drive; FRS/GMRS handheld
  • HAM station: PC + Winlink + VHF/UHF transceiver + VARA FM soundcard modem (or a hardware TNC, terminal node controller)
  • EOC: laptop + VHF/UHF radio + modem to receive
  • License: Technician or higher

Pre-Event Prep

  • Identify and program the nearest RMS gateways
  • Test the VARA FM connection monthly
  • Program the stake voice net (repeater + simplex) and ward FRS/GMRS channels
  • Keep USB drives on hand at the EOC

Voice lane at Contingency: FRS/GMRS to the Team Leader, stake net to the EOC (next slide). VARA FM also carries the SITREP up

Contingency is where the HAM license and equipment investment pays off. VARA FM, the free soundcard modem, is the easiest modern TNC for beginners and installs alongside Winlink Express. The key prep work is knowing your nearest RMS gateways and having tested the VARA FM connection.

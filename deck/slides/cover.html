EMCOMM Planning Document

October 2026

Welcome. This deck covers Church of Jesus Christ EMCOMM, the system built specifically for The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints emergency response — from field volunteer data capture through Winlink radio delivery to the Stake EOC and the two lines above it, the council line (Area Seventy) and the welfare line (WSRM, AWSRM). It also covers the communications PACE plan: what happens as infrastructure degrades from full internet down to radio and runners, who does what at each level, and how the stake stays connected to the lines above it.

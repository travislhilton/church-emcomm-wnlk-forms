The 3 Forms

Church of Jesus Christ EMCOMM

Each form has two versions: a Winlink template (for HAM operators) and a Worker Form (for any volunteer on any device)

Disaster Report

PD60000184

Documents damage, injuries, deaths, and needs at a specific location.

  • Missionaries, members, Church buildings, community
  • Injuries, deaths, homes damaged or destroyed
  • Volunteers deployed, hours and activities; anticipated needs (local, neighboring stakes, headquarters)
  • GPS (Global Positioning System) coordinates auto-captured

Routes to: Satellite EOC → Stake EOC → the welfare line (WSRM, AWSRM)

Supply Order

PD10052205

Requests supplies, food, equipment, and services for relief operations.

  • 65 catalog items with quantity and unit, plus local items
  • Delivery contact, address and requested date
  • Project number and name (internal use)
  • Requester and Area Welfare Manager signatures

Routes to: Team Leader → Stake EOC → the welfare line (for approval)

Work Crew Log

PD10052209

Tracks volunteer crew members, hours worked, and tasks completed.

  • Up to 16 crew members per log
  • Hours auto-totaled by the form
  • Task and location details
  • Crew Chief signs off

Routes to: Crew Chief → ERD → Stake EOC

Each form becomes a record in the EOC Message Center and rolls up the chain. For drills, tick THIS IS AN EXERCISE on any form

Three forms only — matching exactly the three Church PDFs already approved for use. The Worker Forms run in any browser with no installation. The Winlink templates run inside Winlink Express on the HAM operator's station.

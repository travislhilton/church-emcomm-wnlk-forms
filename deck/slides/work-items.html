Leadership looks at the counts; operators work the list. Copy as text into an email or Winlink message, or as a spreadsheet.

Where it goes

  • UP = waiting on the line above. The item rides the next roll-up and lands open on the EOC above, naming yours, until they close it.
  • SITREP Section I: what waits above, what is being worked here, the decisions recorded this period.
  • Overview shows the open count; the wall display lists the high-priority items and flags any with no owner.
  • Shift handoff and standalone mode carry the list; the simulator injects items and reads the list in its hot wash.
Work Items is where the EOC decides, not just records. Every report adds its items on its own, so nothing a form asked for is forgotten; operators own them, move them, and write the decision in. UP sends a decision up the chain with the next roll-up; Section I of the SITREP carries the open list to the lines above the stake.

The Challenge

Paper Forms Fail When
Disasters Are Worst

Paper-Only Problems

  • Forms get lost, wet, or illegible
  • No copy stays with the sender
  • Routing up the chain is manual
  • No searchable record at the EOC
  • Supply orders can't be tracked

This System Solves It

  • Any volunteer fills a form on their phone
  • Data saved locally — no internet needed
  • HAM operators relay via Winlink radio
  • Stake EOC tracks every report; a situation report (SITREP) goes up to the Area
  • Works with full internet — or none at all

All 3 forms mirror the official Church PDF (Portable Document Format) forms (PD60000184, PD10052205, PD10052209)

The core insight: paper forms work until they don't. This digital system mirrors the exact same Church-approved forms but adds resilience at every layer — local saves, offline operation, and radio transport when cell and internet are gone.

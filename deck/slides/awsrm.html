Two Lines Above the Stake: Council and Welfare

The coordinating council (the Area Seventy: decisions) and the welfare line (the local Welfare & Self-Reliance Manager, then the AWSRM: supplies, approvals, reporting), both fed at every PACE level

What the welfare line receives

Disaster Reports

Location, injuries, homes damaged, missionaries, anticipated needs

Supply Orders

Items requested, quantities, delivery address, requested date

SITREP from Stake EOC

To both lines, on the interval leadership sets; header carries the PACE level

How the two lines respond

  • Welfare line: reply to Supply Orders by Winlink or email (approve / deny / modify)
  • Coordinate with Church headquarters for large-scale resources
  • Direct inter-stake resource sharing
  • Council (Area Seventy): cross-stake decisions
  • Authorize additional Helping Hands deployments

Stake → Area by PACE Level

P/A

SITREP by email, forms by Winlink Telnet over any internet, including Starlink. Phone or Wi-Fi calling for urgent.

C

Stake sends by VARA FM or VARA HF into the Winlink CMS; the welfare line picks it up by Telnet or radio, whichever the Area has. Voice: VHF/UHF to the Council / Area EOC station, then HF, or via the county EOC amateur station.

E

Stake liaison at the county EOC passes the SITREP by county Starlink or sat phone; or courier to the Council / Area EOC.

Winlink stores messages until picked up, so the field hop and the hop above the stake can be at different levels at once.

Council / Area EOC — only if one is opened

  • Opened by the council or welfare region. Choice [3]; Setup → level Council / Area EOC; roster lists stakes
  • Stake roll-ups merge themselves from the Drop Folder (email) or the Winlink watcher; SITREP Section H shows which stake has gone quiet
  • Open any row; answer each Supply Order to that stake EOC’s email or Winlink address
  • Equipment: Winlink Express + any connection; VHF/UHF or HF for Contingency; the Area Specialist runs the station

Welfare line = resource gateway, council = decision line — every SITREP, Supply Order and Disaster Report reaches them on whichever path is open

Above the stake there is no standing Area EOC: the coordinating council is the Area Seventy and his stakes (reached by email), and the welfare line is the local Welfare and Self-Reliance Manager reporting to the AWSRM in the Area office. The SITREP goes to both; forms and approvals ride the welfare line. The welfare line is where the Church's broader resource network gets activated. Our system ensures they receive structured, machine-readable data rather than informal phone calls. The hop from the Stake EOC to the lines above has its own PACE ladder, and the thing to remember is that Winlink decouples the two ends: the stake can send by radio while the AWSRM reads by internet. At Emergency the stake liaison at the county EOC is the path, using the county's satellite phone or Starlink.
